Fact 1: Amazing Octopus

Octopuses have three hearts, 9 brains, and blue blood.

How Smart are Octopuses?

Octopuses can solve puzzles, open jars, and even recognize human faces.

Fact 2: Lightning strikes

Lightining strikes the Earth 100 times every single second. That is roughly 8 million lightning strikes per day!

How hot is lightning?

A single bolt of lightning is around 30,000 kelvin- five times hotter than the surface of the sun!

Can lightning strike twice?

Yes! Lightning absolutely can and does strike the same place more than once, especially tall structures like towers and trees

Fact 3: The Deep Ocean

More than 80% of the world's oceans remain unexplored. Scientists believe there are millions of species living in the deep sea that we have never discovered.

How deep does the ocean go?

The deepest point on Earth is the Mariana Trench, which reaches a depth of about 11 kilometers- deep enough to fit Mount Everest with room to spare

Planet Earth – Quick Guide

Our planet is full of incredible records, species, and facts. Here is a quick guide using lists!

Steps to Protect Our Planet:

  1. Switch of the Lights when you leave a room
  2. Use a reusable water bottle every day
  3. Walk or cycle instead of travelling by car
  4. Plant a tree in your neighborhood
  5. Spread awareness – talk to your friends!

Incredible Natural Wonders

  • The Grand Canyon in the United States
  • The Great Barrier Reef in Australia
  • Mount Everest in Nepal
  • The Amazon Rainforest in South America
  • The Northern Lights in the Arctic Circle

Key Terms:

Climate Change
Long-term changes in temperature, precipitation, and other atmospheric conditions on Earth.
Deforestation
The clearing or thinning of forests by humans, often for agriculture or urban development.
Renewable Energy
Energy derived from natural resources that are replenished on a human timescale, such as sunlight, wind, and water.
Biodiversity
The variety of life in the world or in a particular habitat or ecosystem.
